Objetivo de hoy

Gustos, planes y pedir comida.

Hoy vas a aprender a decir lo que te gusta, contar tus planes de fin de semana, y a pedir comida en inglés — con un role-play completo en McDonald's.

Gramática de hoy

Tres formas que vas a usar hoy.

like / love / hate + -ing "I like cooking." "I hate waiting."
I'd like + sustantivo/verbo "I'd like a coffee." "I'd like to order, please."
I'm going to + verbo "I'm going to visit my family this weekend."
Warm-up · 5 min

Repaso + algo nuevo.

Di una oración sobre tu rutina (de la clase pasada) y una oración nueva con "I like" o "I don't like".

Antes de empezar

Tus frases clave.

Tú (cliente)
  • Can I get a... / I'd like a...
  • Can I have that as a combo?
  • Medium, please.
  • For here or to go?
  • That's all, thank you.
Quien atiende (profesor)
  • What can I get for you?
  • Would you like a combo with that?
  • What size would you like?
  • For here or to go?
  • That'll be $[cantidad]. Cash or card?
Diálogo modelo

Así se arma un pedido.

CajeroHi, welcome to McDonald's! What can I get for you?
ClienteHi, I'd like a Big Mac, please.
CajeroWould you like to make that a combo?
ClienteYes, please.
CajeroWhat size fries and drink would you like?
ClienteMedium, please.
CajeroFor here or to go?
ClienteTo go, please.
CajeroThat'll be $110. Cash or card?
ClienteCard, please.
Repite 2-3 veces cambiando el pedido, hasta pedir sin leer

Cierre integrador · 10 min
